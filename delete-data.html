Rupo

Delete your Rupo data

Rupo for Android (package dev.gunal.rupo), by Gunal Seenivasagan.
Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short. Rupo has no accounts. Almost everything it keeps stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is the shop names you chose to share with other Rupo users, and you can take those back yourself, in the app, at any time.

Shop names you shared

If you turned on Share and see shop names, take back every shop name your phone shared:

  1. Open Rupo and go to Settings.
  2. Under Shop names, turn off Share and see shop names.
  3. Rupo takes the names back from its server the next time your phone is online, usually within minutes. Until then Settings says "Taking back what this phone shared, the next time it is online".

To take back just one name, open that payment, untick Share this name with other Rupo users under "Paid to" (or clear "Paid to"), and save.

If Rupo is no longer on your phone

Rupo's server cannot tell which names came from which phone, so it cannot find yours by itself. Write to gunal2002@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Rupo shop names", and for each name: the name you gave, the payee as your bank's SMS printed it, and roughly where you paid. You do not need to say who you are.

For each, one vote with that payee, place and name is removed within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done. Every vote for the same name at the same place is alike, so that removes what your phone had sent.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: for each name, everything the server held: the payee as your bank printed it, the place as a square about 150 m across, the name, the day, and the code your phone made for that place.
  • Kept by Rupo: nothing from your phone. A name that other Rupo users also gave the same shop stays, as theirs.
  • Kept for 1 day: Supabase, which runs Rupo's server, keeps a log of each request, with the IP address it came from, for 1 day, and then deletes it. Rupo cannot delete it sooner.

Everything on your phone

Your payments, their places, your notes and names, and your friends never leave the phone. To delete all of it, uninstall Rupo, or open Android Settings, Apps, Rupo, Storage, and choose Clear storage. This cannot be undone. If you shared shop names, turn sharing off first and let the phone go online, so that they are taken back before the app is gone.

More on what Rupo reads, keeps and sends is in the privacy policy.
