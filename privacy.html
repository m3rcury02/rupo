Rupo Privacy Policy

Applies to the Rupo app for Android (package dev.gunal.rupo).
Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short. Rupo reads the SMS your bank and card send for each payment, and notes where your phone was when each payment was made, so that you can look back on your payments later.

Who is responsible

Rupo is made by Gunal Seenivasagan, an individual developer. For any question about privacy or this policy, write to gunal2002@gmail.com.

What Rupo reads, and why

WhatWhyWhat is kept
SMS messages To find the messages that banks and card companies send about payments, so that your payments can be listed for review. Rupo reads each SMS as it arrives, also while the app is closed, and can read the messages already in your inbox. For a bank or card transaction message: the text of the message, its sender, the amount, the payee as the bank printed it, the last digits of the account or card, the reference number and the date. A message that looks like a payment but that Rupo can't read for certain is shown in the payments list, marked "check this". A bank message with an amount that Rupo skips is kept for 60 days under Settings, Bank SMS, "Skipped SMS".
Location, including in the background Rupo collects location data to enable noting where each payment was made, even when the app is closed or not in use. It is also read when you tap "Use current location" to set Home or Office, and when you tap "Try a fix now". When you open a payment, its position is shown on a map drawn by Google Maps, and the shops near it are listed from OpenStreetMap. The position, its accuracy and the time it was read, saved with the payment. The positions you set as Home and Office.
Photos in the camera folder you choose (optional) To show you the photos you took around the time of a payment or an outing, as a reminder of what it was. Nothing.
Contacts (optional) To suggest names while you type a friend's name on an outing. The name of a friend you choose to add.

Rupo also keeps what you enter yourself: the notes you write about payments, the friends you add, who was at each outing and their shares, the place names you type, and the payees and merchant names you choose to hide.

Who your data is shared with

When you open a payment, its position is shown on a map drawn by Google Maps. To draw the map, Google receives the position, your IP address, information about your device and the app (including an identifier the map uses to count its users), and crash data if the map fails. Google's handling of this is covered by Google's privacy policy.

When you open a payment that has a position, the position is also sent to Photon, a search over OpenStreetMap run by komoot, to list the named shops near it. Photon receives the position, how far around it to look, and your IP address, which any request over the internet carries.

Rupo does not currently share your data with anyone else.

When you tap "Open in Maps", the position is passed to the maps app on your phone. When you tap "Copy summary", a line about the outing is put on your phone's clipboard.

When you tap "Send to the developer", Rupo opens your email app with a message to the developer that contains the SMS exactly as received, its sender and the time it arrived, the app version, and what Rupo read and what you tapped. The developer uses it to teach Rupo that message's format, and keeps it with the app's tests. To have it deleted, write to the contact address below.

Rupo gets the phone's position from Google Play services. Google's handling of location is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Rupo keeps your data until you remove it. To delete everything Rupo has kept, either:

  • uninstall the app, or
  • open Android Settings, Apps, Rupo, Storage, and choose "Clear storage".

Both remove all of it at once and cannot be undone.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings, Apps, Rupo, Permissions. Without SMS access nothing new is recorded. Without location access payments are recorded without a place.

Security

The data sits in storage that Android reserves for Rupo and that other apps cannot read. It is as secure as your phone: anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see it.

Children

Rupo is meant for adults who manage their own bank accounts. It is not directed at children.
